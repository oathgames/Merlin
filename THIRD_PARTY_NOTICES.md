## Not imported

`realkimbarrett/advertising-skills` was evaluated but **not** imported: the
repository ships no LICENSE file, so its contents are all-rights-reserved by
default. The one genuinely additive idea it surfaced, an objection taxonomy
with a logic/proof/emotion rebuttal structure, is long-standing direct-response
canon (Eugene Schwartz, Gary Halbert). It is stated in Merlin independently from
primary sources, not adapted from that repository.
